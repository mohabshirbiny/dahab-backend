Three separate controls sit between the Evolve feed and the price a customer sees. They are not interchangeable.

Price correction

Always on
A fixed amount per gram added to the reference price. It applies while the feed is healthy and updates with every refresh. This corrects the feed against the real market. It is not a revenue line.
