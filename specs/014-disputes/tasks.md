## As built (2026-10-04)

- The customer order carries what the customer may do in its existing `actions` list (`report_problem`,
  `ask_more_time`, `name_proxy`) instead of a separate `can` object, so the spec 012 consumers keep one shape.
- The frozen time given back on resume and the compensation day cap read the application clock
  (`CarbonImmutable::now()`), not the database clock, so they agree with every other deadline and tests can travel.
- The branch that moved an uncollected paid piece back on resume never fires (resume returns to the frozen-from
  state), so it was removed (research R4).
- `dispute_photo` limits and the "compensation needs a dispute or a reason" rule are checked in the Actions, not by
  database constraints.
- Tests are grouped by behaviour under `tests/Feature/Dispute/` plus `ProxyCollectionTest` and `ExtensionRequestTest`
  under `tests/Feature/Order/`; `DisputeConcurrencyTest` puts back the 21K adjustments `workedPrices()` changes.

  `OrderTimeline` now skips `disputed → ready_to_collect`.
